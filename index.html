DNA Replication — 5 Steps

1

Unwinding

Helicase DNA ko kholta hai, replication fork banta hai.

2

Primer

Primase RNA primer banata hai — starting point.

3

Elongation

DNA polymerase III nayi strand banata hai (5′→3′).

4

Okazaki Fragments

Lagging strand par chhote fragments, ligase jodta hai.

5

Two DNAs

Result: 2 identical DNA — semi-conservative replication.

NEET fact: Chargaff's Rule — A = T aur G = C. Isliye (A+G) = (T+C). DNA ka ek turn = 3.4 nm, 10 base pairs.

Test 04 • Breathing and Exchange of Gases

TermValue / Fact
Tidal Volume (TV)500 mL — normal breath
Vital Capacity (VC)4,600 mL (TV+IRV+ERV)
Residual Volume (RV)1,100–1,200 mL
Total Lung Capacity~5,800 mL
Respiratory rate12–16 / min

Yaad rakhne wali baatein

• Breathing: inspiration = diaphragm contracts, flat hota hai; expiration = relax.
• Gas exchange alveoli me diffusion se — O₂ blood me, CO₂ bahar.
• O₂ transport: 97% haemoglobin se (oxyhaemoglobin). CO₂: 70% bicarbonate, 20–25% carbamino-haemoglobin.
• Oxygen–haemoglobin curve sigmoid hoti hai.
• Disorders: Asthma, Emphysema (cigarette smoking), Occupational lung disease (silicosis/asbestosis).

Air → Nose → Pharynx → Larynx → Trachea → Bronchi → Bronchioles → Alveoli

Yaad rakhne wali baatein

• Blood groups: ABO + Rh. Universal donor O⁻, universal recipient AB⁺.
• Heart: 4 chambers, SA node pacemaker, AV node, Bundle of His.
• Double circulation — pulmonary + systemic.
• Lymph: tissue fluid, returns to blood, immunity.
• Disorders: Hypertension, Coronary artery disease, Angina, Heart failure.

Right Atrium → Right Ventricle → Lungs → Left Atrium → Left Ventricle → Body

Test 04 • Excretory Products and their Elimination

StepWhere / How
UltrafiltrationGlomerulus → Bowman's capsule, GFR ~125 mL/min
ReabsorptionPCT: glucose, amino acids, 70% water; Loop of Henle: water + salts
SecretionDCT + collecting duct: H⁺, K⁺, drugs
Urine~1–1.5 L/day, 95% water, urea main waste
ADHWater reabsorption ↑, urine volume ↓
AldosteroneNa⁺ reabsorption ↑

Yaad rakhne wali baatein

• Nephron = kidney ki functional unit (~1 million per kidney).
• Urea ornithine cycle (liver) se banta hai; ammonia most toxic, uric acid least toxic (birds).
• Counter-current mechanism: Henle's loop + vasa recta — concentrated urine.
• Micturition: bladder se urine bahar — neural control.
• Disorders: Uremia, Kidney failure, Kidney stones, Dialysis / transplant.

Afferent Arteriole → Glomerulus → PCT → Loop of Henle → DCT → Collecting Duct

Test 04 (Repeat) • Cell Cycle and Cell Division

G1

Mitosis ki taiyaari.

Mitosis

PMAT: Prophase → Metaphase → Anaphase → Telophase.

Meiosis

Reduction

Meiosis I (reductional) + Meiosis II (equational). Crossing over = Prophase I, pachytene.

Mitosis: 2 identical diploid cells (growth/repair). Meiosis: 4 haploid gametes (variation). NEET: metaphase me chromosomes equator par, anaphase me sister chromatids alag.
